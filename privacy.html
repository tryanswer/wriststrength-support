WristStrength privacy / 力量记录隐私

2026-10-15

English

WristStrength stores your exercise names, routines, weights, reps, set types, completion and session timestamps on your device. It requires no account and does not read health sensors. The app includes no advertising, analytics, developer-operated cloud storage or subscription.

Logging starts only after you agree to local storage. Refusing keeps logging disabled; Privacy remains available. Apple and Wear OS first release provide no watch backup, restore or paired transfer. Uninstalling or losing the device can lose watch-local records. Phone document functions save/import only that phone's independent records; they do not back up the watch.

On Huawei, the first-use privacy prompt opens the full policy before agreement; Refuse closes the app without deleting existing records. Huawei alone offers optional workout JSON export/import through a temporary foreground local Wi-Fi link. Use a trusted private network, keep the watch page open, and close the link after transfer. The link expires after ten minutes. Exported archives contain private workout records.

Privacy → Delete all data removes this device's local business records and resets agreement. Files already saved/shared outside the app remain under your control and must be deleted separately. The app has no cross-device deletion or backup guarantee. This is a workout log, not medical advice or a measurement of calories or health.

Support messages are optional. If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to send, and use it to handle your request. Avoid sending workout archives unless needed for your request. Contact support@fatclaw.com.

This static support website has no added analytics. Its hosting provider may process IP addresses and access logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

中文

力量记录将动作名称、训练模板、重量、次数、组类型、完成状态及训练时间保存在您的设备上。无需账号，不读取健康传感器。应用不包含广告、分析统计、开发者运营的云端存储或订阅。

同意本地保存后才启用训练记录；拒绝后不启用记录，仍可查看隐私页面。Apple 与 Wear OS 首版不提供手表备份、恢复或配对传输。卸载应用或设备丢失可能导致手表本机记录丢失。手机本地文件功能仅保存、导入该手机独立保存的记录，不会备份手表。

Huawei 首次使用的隐私提示会在同意前打开完整政策；拒绝会退出应用，不删除已有记录。仅 Huawei 提供可选训练 JSON 导出、导入，通过临时前台本地 Wi-Fi 链接进行。请使用可信私人网络，保持手表页面打开，传输后关闭链接；链接十分钟后到期。导出的文件包含私人训练记录。

隐私→删除全部数据会移除本机业务记录并重置同意状态。已保存或分享到应用外的文件由您自行管理，需另外删除。应用不保证跨设备删除或备份。本应用用于记录训练，不提供医疗建议，也不测量卡路里或健康状态。

联系支持是可选的。您发邮件时，我们会收到您的地址及您选择提供的信息，用于处理请求。除非排查需要，请勿发送训练文件。联系：support@fatclaw.com。
