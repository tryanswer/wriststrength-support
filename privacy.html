WristStrength privacy / 力量记录隐私

2026-10-13

English

WristStrength stores your exercise names, routines, weights, reps, set types, completion and session timestamps on your device. It requires no account and does not read health sensors. The app includes no advertising, analytics, developer-operated cloud storage or subscription.

Logging starts only after you agree to local storage. Refusing keeps logging disabled; Privacy remains available. Optional export sends the selected workout archive through the operating system's paired-device service, or a temporary local Wi-Fi link on Huawei. Paired services may queue a file until delivery. On Wear OS, Google Play services uses Bluetooth when connected and may relay an end-to-end encrypted archive through Google servers otherwise. Setting up that pairing may require a Google account. Use a trusted private network, and close the local link after transfer.

Privacy → Delete all data removes local business records and resets agreement. Apple/Wear companion received copies must also be deleted on that companion; files already saved/shared outside the app remain under your control. Operating-system transfers, device backups and recipient storage are controlled by their providers. This is a workout log, not medical advice or a measurement of calories or health.

Support messages are optional. If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to send, and use it to handle your request. Avoid sending workout archives unless needed for your request. Contact support@fatclaw.com.

This static support website has no added analytics. Its hosting provider may process IP addresses and access logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

中文

力量记录将动作名称、训练模板、重量、次数、组类型、完成状态及训练时间保存在您的设备上。无需账号，不读取健康传感器。应用不包含广告、分析统计、开发者运营的云端存储或订阅。

同意本地保存后才启用训练记录；拒绝后不启用记录，仍可查看隐私页面。可选导出通过系统配对设备服务传送所选训练文件，或在 Huawei 上使用临时本地 Wi-Fi 链接。配对服务可能排队等待接收。Wear OS 的 Google Play 服务在连接时使用蓝牙，其他情况下可能通过 Google 服务器中转端到端加密的文件；设置配对可能需要 Google 账号。请使用可信私人网络，传输后关闭本地链接。

隐私→删除全部数据会移除本机业务记录并重置同意状态。Apple／Wear 手机伴侣中收到的副本需在伴侣上另外删除；已保存或分享到应用外的文件由您自行管理。系统传输、设备备份及接收设备的存储由相应服务商控制。本应用用于记录训练，不提供医疗建议，也不测量卡路里或健康状态。

联系支持是可选的。您发邮件时，我们会收到您的地址及您选择提供的信息，用于处理请求。除非排查需要，请勿发送训练文件。联系：support@fatclaw.com。

本静态支持网站未添加分析统计。托管方可能处理 IP 地址及访问日志，详见 GitHub 隐私声明。
